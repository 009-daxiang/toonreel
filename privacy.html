Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

YT688 (“we”, “our”, “the site”) operates a free streaming service for animated micro dramas. This policy explains what data we handle and how third-party advertising works on the site.

1. Information we store on your device

We store a small amount of data locally in your browser (localStorage) to remember your preferences: selected language, favorite dramas, watch progress and unlocked episodes. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us. Clearing your browser data resets these preferences.

2. Third-party advertising

The site is free because it is supported by advertising. We work with third-party advertising partners (such as Google AdSense and video ad networks) that may:

  • Serve ads based on your visit to this and other websites;
  • Use cookies, web beacons or similar technologies to measure ad performance;
  • Use a unique advertising identifier to personalize or measure ads, where permitted by your consent choices.

These partners process data under their own privacy policies. You can review Google's policy at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

3. Your choices

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings or via youronlinechoices.com (EU).
  • Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies in settings. Blocking ad cookies may make ads less relevant but the site remains fully usable.
  • Mobile devices: you can limit ad tracking in your system privacy settings (Limit Ad Tracking / reset advertising ID).

4. Children

The service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Video content

Video streams are delivered from our content delivery partners purely to enable playback. We do not sell your personal viewing data.

6. Changes

We may update this policy as the service evolves. Changes are published on this page with an updated date.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us on Telegram (@WYT688) or LINE (daxiang0008).
